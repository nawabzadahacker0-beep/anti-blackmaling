Emergency Protocol By Nawab Zada Hacker

Jab Koi Aapko Private Video ya Photos Leak Karne Ki Dhamki De Toh Kya Karein?

Online blackmailing aur cyber harassment ka shikaar hotay waqt sab se pehli aur ahem baat yeh hai ke aapne bilkul ghabrana nahi hai. Blackmailers hamesha victim ke khauf aur sharam ka faida uthate hain. Kabi bhi blackmailer ko paise (ransom) ya mazeed koi tasweer ya audio mat bhejein. Yaad rakhein, ek dafa paise dene se blackmailer ki talab khatam nahi hoti balke wo mazeed blackmailing shuru kar deta hai.

Aapko foran tamam cheezon ka digital saboot (evidence) mehfooz karna hoga. Isme blackmailer ke tamam WhatsApp messages, audio notes, calls ka record, profile links, aur transaction details (Easypaisa / JazzCash / Bank Account) ke clear screenshots shamil hain. Blackmailer ko foran block na karein jab tak aap tamam saboot mehfooz na kar lein. Iske baad hamari website ke Blackmail Report Portal par data submit karein ya FIA Cyber Crime Wing (NR3C) ko foran report darj karwayein.

Financial Fraud Prevention By Nawab Zada Hacker

Fake EasyPaisa & JazzCash Payment Screenshots Aur Receipts Ki Pehchan Kaise Karein?

Aaj kal Pakistan me online business karne walon aur aam logon ke sath fake payment slips ke zariye fraud aam ho chuka hai. Scammers aisi spoof applications aur APK tools istemal karte hain jo asil jesi dikhne wali EasyPaisa, JazzCash ya Bank Transfer receipts foran generate kar deti hain. Yahan tak ke woh fake SMS gateways ke zariye aapke number par 8558 ya 3737 jese numbers se milte julte fake messages bhi bhejte hain.

Kisi bhi shakhs ke bheje gaye screenshot ya SMS par kabhi andha aitbaar na karein. Jab tak aap khud apni official EasyPaisa ya JazzCash application open karke apna actual account balance aur transaction history check na kar lein, tab tak kisi ko koi product, crypto, ya service transfer na karein. Agar fraud ho chuka ho toh us number aur account ko State Bank aur Cyber Crime Cell me foran freeze karwayein.

Account Security By Nawab Zada Hacker

WhatsApp Aur Social Media Accounts Ko Hack Hone Se Bachane Ka Mukammal Tareeqa

Aksar auqat WhatsApp accounts social engineering aur call forwarding tricks ke zariye hijack kiye jatay hain. Hacker aapko call karke koi lalach deta hai aur kehta hai ke *21* ya koi code dial karein, ya phir aapke number par aane wala 6-digit verification code mangta hai. Kabhi bhi apna OTP code kisi bhi shakhs ya call par share na karein, chahe samne wala khud ko kisi helpline ka numainda hi kyun na zahir kare.

Apne WhatsApp ki settings me ja kar foran Two-Step Verification on karein aur ek secret 6-digit PIN code set karein sath hi apni recovery email lazmi attach karein. Rozana ki bunyad par WhatsApp Settings me Linked Devices check karein taake koi aapka WhatsApp web par monitor na kar raha ho. Yeh asool apne Facebook, Instagram aur Gmail par bhi lazmi implement karein.

Legal Framework By Nawab Zada Hacker

PECA Act 2016: Pakistan Me Cyber Crime Aur Blackmailing Ke Khilaf Qanoon Aur Sazayein

Pakistan me electronic crimes ki rok-thaam ke liye Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) 2016 nafizul amal hai. Is qanoon ki Section 20, 21 aur 24 ke mutabiq kisi bhi shakhs ki ijazat ke baghair uski private tasweer, video ya audio viral karna ya blackmailing ke liye istemal karna aik gheenona aur na-qabil-e-zamanat jurm hai. Is jurm ki saza 5 saal tak qaid-e-ba-mushaqqat aur 50 Lakh rupay tak jurmana hai.

Agar aap kisi bhi kisam ki online harassment, blackmailing ya fake account ki zad me hain toh qanoon aapki mukammal hifazat karta hai. FIA NR3C (National Response Centre for Cyber Crime) victims ki identity ko 100% secret rakh kar culprits ke khilaf FIR darj karti hai aur unke mobile phones aur devices zabt karke forensics test karwati hai. Hamari team is legal process me aapko muft technical guide faraham karti hai.
