FIA Cyber Crime Wing (NR3C) Report Procedure

Agar aapko FIA mein complaint darj karni hai to darj zail 3 steps follow karein:

1

Saboot Mehfooz Karein (Evidence Preservation)

Blackmailer ke tamam messages, voice notes, calls ka record aur social media profile links save karein. Block karne se pehle clear screenshots lein.

2

FIA Online Complaint Portal

FIA ki official website complaint.fia.gov.pk par ja kar online form submit karein.

3

FIA Helpline (Toll-Free 1991)

Emergency ke liye FIA Cyber Crime Wing ki official toll-free helpline 1991 par call karein aur apna masla darj karwayein.

Important Notice & Technical Assistance

Agar aapko foran madad chahiye to pehle hamari team se contact karein. Hum aapko safe forensics aur FIA complaint file karne mein complete technical help faraham karenge. Aapka data 100% confidential aur safe rahega.
