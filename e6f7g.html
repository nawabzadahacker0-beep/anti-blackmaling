FIA Helpline (1991) Par Call Karke Kya Kehna Hai?

Jab aap 1991 milayein ge to representative aap se mukhtasar sawalat pooche ga. Bilkul pur-sukoon ho kar darj zail baatein batayein:

1. Apna Masla Bayan Karein: "Mera naam [Aapka Naam] hai. Mujhe WhatsApp / Facebook / Instagram par ek shakhs [Blackmailer ka number ya account] ke zariye meri private video/tasweer leak karne ki dhamkiyan de kar paise mang raha hai / blackmail kar raha hai."

2. Saboot Ka Batayein: "Mere paas blackmailer ke tamam chat screenshots, call recordings, aur Easypaisa / Bank account number ka record mehfooz hai."

3. Diary Number Lein: Call khatam hone par representative se kahein: "Mujhe meri complaint ka Reference / Diary Number note karwa dein" taake aap baad mein case trace kar sakein.

PECA 2016 Act Ki Ahem Dafaat (Sections)

Section 20 (Dignity of Natural Person):

Kisi ki ijazat ke baghair fake account banana ya badnaam karna. (3 Saal Saza / 10 Lakh Jurmana).

Section 21 (Modesty & Explicit Content):

Private video ya tasweer viral karne ki dhamki ya blackmailing. (5 Saal Saza / 50 Lakh Jurmana).

Section 24 (Cyber Stalking & Harassment):

Bar bar call, messages ya internet par shadeed preshan karna. (3 Saal Saza / 10 Lakh Jurmana).
