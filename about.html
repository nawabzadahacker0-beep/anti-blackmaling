Cyber Forensics & Anti-Extortion Specialist

The Anti-Blackmailing site is Pakistan's first dedicated platform providing confidential support to victims of online blackmail, social media harassment, and financial fraud. We ensure a safe environment where individuals can seek help without fear of exposure.

Our mission is to empower citizens across Pakistan and globally who are targeted using private photos or videos as a tool for harassment. We guarantee "Your Information Remains Completely Hidden — Your Privacy Is Our Priority."

Founded by Nawab Zada Hacker, a certified ethical security researcher, this initiative specializes in digital forensics, evidence preservation, and legal guidance under Pakistan's PECA Act 2016. We collaborate with victims to document evidence and guide them through official complaint procedures with FIA Cyber Crime Wing NR3C.

Our commitment is to eradicate cyber extortion by providing rapid response, technical support, and complete anonymity. Every case is handled with the highest level of confidentiality and professional integrity.
