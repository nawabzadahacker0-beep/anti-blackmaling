FIA Cyber Crime Wing (NR3C) Report Procedure

1

Preserve All Evidence

Save all messages, voice notes, call recordings, and social media profile URLs of the blackmailer. Take screenshots before blocking.

2

FIA Official Online Portal

Visit FIA official website complaint.fia.gov.pk to verify CNIC and lodge your complaint online.

3

FIA Helpline (Toll-Free 1991)

Call toll-free helpline 1991 from any mobile network to register an emergency complaint with FIA Cyber Crime Wing.
