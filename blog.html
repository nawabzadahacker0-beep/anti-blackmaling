Emergency Protocol

What To Do If Someone Threatens To Leak Your Private Content?

Do not pay the blackmailer under any circumstances. Payment increases their demands. Immediately preserve all evidence including chats, call logs, and social media profiles. File a report through the Anti-Blackmailing Portal or directly with FIA Cyber Crime Wing for legal action.

Financial Fraud Prevention

How To Detect Fake Easypaisa & JazzCash Payment Screenshots

Scammers use fake payment apps to generate fraudulent receipts. Never confirm transactions based on SMS or screenshots. Always verify payments by checking your official banking app balance before releasing products or services.

Account Security

How To Secure Your WhatsApp Account: Enable Two-Step Verification

Protect your WhatsApp with a Six-Digit PIN in Settings > Account > Two-Step Verification. Never share OTP codes or call forwarding codes with anyone. Regularly audit Linked Devices to detect unauthorized access.

Legal Framework

PECA Act 2016: Cyber Crime Laws and Punishments in Pakistan

Under Pakistan's Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016, unauthorized sharing of private images can lead to 5 years imprisonment and fines up to 5 million PKR. Learn the legal process for filing complaints with FIA NR3C.
